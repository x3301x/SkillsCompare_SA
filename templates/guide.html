How SkillsCompare SA works

If you are buying training

  1. Search

    Enter the skill you need, how many learners, where they are and whether you want onsite, blended or online delivery. Use the filters to narrow by price, duration, provider type or verified credentials.

  2. Read the results

    Every programme shows the same fields: price and what it is based on, duration, format, location and capacity. Open “Why this match” to see exactly which rules produced the order. Programmes without a published price show Request Quote.

  3. Compare up to three

    Select “Add to compare” on any result. The comparison page puts the programmes side by side and works out an estimated total for your number of learners.

  4. Send an enquiry

    Choose a programme, add your dates, credential requirements and budget, then give your contact details and consent. Only that provider receives them and they reply to you directly. Contracting, invoicing and payment happen between you and the provider.

If you are a training provider

  1. Register

    Create a provider account with your organisation’s details. New providers are approved by an administrator before anything goes live.

  2. List your programmes

    Add each programme from your dashboard with its price basis, duration, format, location and capacity. New listings are reviewed before they appear in search.

  3. Submit accreditation evidence

    Upload proof of any accreditation you hold. It shows as “Under review” until an administrator checks it and records the authority, method and date.

  4. Respond to leads

    Enquiries appear in your dashboard with the learner’s requirements and the consent they gave. Update each lead’s status as you follow up.

What the credential labels mean

Verified by SkillsCompare means an administrator checked it and recorded the authority, method and date. Under review means evidence has been submitted but not yet checked. Unverified claim means the provider says it holds the credential and no check has been recorded. Non-accredited short course means no accreditation is claimed.
